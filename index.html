Before you ask

Is GTA 6 confirmed for PC?

Not yet. As of now, Rockstar has only confirmed PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S for November 19, 2026. Rockstar's last two major releases (GTA V, RDR2) both came to PC roughly 12–18 months after console launch, which is the pattern most analysts expect to repeat.

Where do your predicted specs come from?

We cross-reference the RAGE engine's requirements history, the confirmed PS5/Xbox Series X hardware floor, and how demanding the environments shown in official trailers appear to be. It's an estimate, clearly labeled as one — never presented as an official Rockstar document.

Will this page update when specs are official?

Yes — that's the entire reason this exists. The scan tool and the table above will be swapped to Rockstar's real, published numbers the day they're announced.
